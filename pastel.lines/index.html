Shapes on blocksEach color gets its own symbol, so you don't have to tell colors apart.

How to play

Tap a block, then tap an empty square to slide it there. Line up 5 or more of the same color (across, down or diagonal) to clear them. Every move that doesn't clear adds 3 new blocks. When the board fills up, the game is over. You earn a power-up as your score climbs, and a bonus one for clearing 7 or more at once.
