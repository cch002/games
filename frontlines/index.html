    ◀ Arcade
    FRONTLINES

    Grow your territory, outmuscle your neighbours and rule the map. Pick a start, set your attack ratio, and push.

    How to play
    • Pick a start. When the map appears, tap anywhere on land to place your capital. You have a few seconds to move it.
    • Attack by tapping land next to you. Grey land is unclaimed and cheap; another nation's land costs more the denser their troops are.
    • The attack ratio slider sets what share of your troops each tap sends. Troops regrow fastest when you are about half full.
    • Tap land across water and a boat sails your troops there (up to 3 at once).
    • Gold accrues with territory. Build Cities for more troops and gold, Posts to harden a border, and a Silo to launch Atom bombs.
    • Tap one of your outgoing attack chips to retreat and recover most of those troops.
    • Own 80% of the land, or be the last one standing, to win.
    • Drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom. Keys: 1–0 ratio, C D S N build, H home, Space pause.
